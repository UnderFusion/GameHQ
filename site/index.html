What is GameHQ?

GameHQ 0.7.1 adds automatic updates, HDR-aware capture, a standard per-user Windows installer, and Playnite integration. Production-signed update manifests protect authenticity while failed updates can roll back safely.

GameHQ brings one coherent capture workflow to PC gaming. Tap one button for a screenshot, hold it after something memorable happens to save recent gameplay, and open a gallery above the running game without alt-tabbing. Captures are identified, grouped, and filed automatically per game.

At its heart is a configurable rolling replay buffer: while you play, GameHQ encodes recent gameplay with system audio into crash-safe segments on disk. Hold Share and those previous minutes become a normal H.264 MP4 with a thumbnail and game metadata. Tap Share for an instant PNG or JPEG, or focus a recorded clip and press Share or S to save the exact displayed video frame as a screenshot.

The in-game overlay supports browsing, playback, favorites, reveal, and deletion from a controller. It refreshes immediately after new captures and automatically closes when Windows focus moves to Start, the task switcher, or another application. The desktop gallery adds thumbnail zoom, bulk selection, watched folders, and a unified view of Game Bar, Steam, NVIDIA, and OBS captures without modifying imported files.

GameHQ 0.7.1 includes thirteen live-switchable themes with Obsidian as the fresh-install default and adjustable overlay dimming. Input supports DualSense, XInput, WinMM, keyboard, and safe extra mouse buttons with fully configurable bindings. If HidHide makes a connected Sony controller invisible to applications, Settings explains the cause and can safely add GameHQ to the existing allow-list.

GameHQ is a portable native C++20/Qt 6 app — no Electron, account, telemetry, background service, or game-process injection. It uses Windows Graphics Capture, Media Foundation, and WASAPI directly, and can keep its settings, database, logs, and captures beside the executable.

How it works

1

Play

GameHQ detects the game and spins up the rolling replay buffer in the background.

2

Capture

Tap Share for a screenshot or hold it to save the configured recent-play window.

3

Browse

Press PS or Guide — the overlay gallery opens above the game, fully controller-driven.

4

Return

Circle closes the overlay and hands focus back to the game, right where you left it.

Why GameHQ

In-game overlay gallery

Browse screenshots and clips above the running game with your controller — no alt-tab, no desktop app in your face.

Always-ready replay buffer

Configurable duration, quality, frame rate, and resolution. One hold of Share saves the moment after it happened.

Organized around games

Captures are grouped per game automatically, with favorites that are never auto-deleted and clean per-game folders.

Native & portable

C++20 + Qt 6, no Electron. Runs from a folder, sits quietly in the tray, plays subtle console-style sounds.

Flexible input

DualSense, XInput, WinMM, keyboard, and extra mouse buttons with rebindable actions, gestures, and controller profiles.

Your existing captures too

Watches Game Bar, Steam, NVIDIA and OBS folders so everything lives in one gallery — without touching your files.

Sixteen languages

App, overlay, Settings, tray and installer are localized. GameHQ follows your Windows language and switches instantly, without a restart.

Themes & accessibility

Thirteen themes, Obsidian by default, live switching, textured backdrops, high contrast, and adjustable overlay dimming.

Controller recovery

Detects supported Sony pads hidden by HidHide and offers a safe allow-list fix without disabling remapping software.

Languages

GameHQ is localized across the desktop interface, the in-game overlay, Settings, the tray menu, the installer and the release notes. The language is taken from Windows on first start and can be changed at any time in Settings › General — the change applies immediately, without restarting.
